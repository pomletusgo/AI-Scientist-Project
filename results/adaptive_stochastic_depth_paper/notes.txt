# Title: Adaptive Stochastic Depth: Dynamic Regularization via Online Loss Monitoring
# Experiment description: Modify the Block class to support stochastic depth: during training, with probability p, the block returns its input unchanged (skipping the attention and MLP paths). The probability p is stored as a non-persistent buffer in the model. After each evaluation interval, compute an exponential moving average (EMA) of the validation loss. If the relative improvement of the EMA over the last two evaluations is less than a threshold epsilon, increase p by a fixed delta (up to pmax); otherwise, decrease p by delta (down to 0). The controller updates p at the end of the evaluation step. Train models on all three datasets with this adaptive scheme and compare against baselines: (1) no stochastic depth, (2) fixed constant p (e.g., 0.1, 0.3), (3) a linear schedule from 0 to pmax. Record training/validation losses and the trajectory of p across iterations. Use multiple seeds per dataset.
